Common Questions

Do I need any experience in real estate to apply?

Not at all. This scholarship is designed for people starting from zero. If you're curious about real estate and willing to put in the work, that's all we need.

What exactly does the scholarship cover?

KW Legacy Group covers the cost of your first six months of real estate coursework through Kaplan. You don't pay anything out of pocket for that portion of your education.

What happens after the six months?

The goal is that within six months you've completed your coursework, passed your licensing exam, and are ready to launch your career. Yvette will walk you through the full timeline on your call.

Am I obligated to join KW Legacy Group after getting licensed?

Yvette will be upfront with you on your call about how the scholarship works and what our expectations are. No surprises, ever.

How long is the scheduling call?

Plan for about 30 minutes. It's a real conversation, not a presentation. Yvette wants to hear about you and make sure you leave with every answer you need.

A KWLG agent told me about this. Does that matter?

Yes! Make sure to enter their name in the referral field on the form above. It helps us keep track and make sure they get the credit they deserve.
