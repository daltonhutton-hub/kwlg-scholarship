Do I need any experience in real estate to apply?

None at all. We've had people come in from all kinds of backgrounds. If you're curious about real estate and willing to do the work, that's the only requirement.

What exactly does the scholarship cover?

KW Legacy Group covers your Kaplan coursework for the first six months. Licensing exam fees, background checks, and brokerage dues are separate costs that come later — Yvette will walk you through the full picture on your call so you know exactly what to expect.

What happens after the six months?

The goal is to finish your Kaplan coursework within the six months, then move on to your state exam, background checks, and licensing fees. It's a process, but it's a clear one — and Yvette will map out the full timeline on your call so there are no surprises.

Am I obligated to join KW Legacy Group after getting licensed?

Yvette will be completely upfront with you on your call about how the KW Legacy Group scholarship works and what we hope for on both sides. She doesn't do surprises.

How long is the scheduling call?

Plan for about 20 minutes. Yvette keeps it focused — she wants to hear about you, answer every question you have, and make sure you walk away knowing exactly what the next step looks like.

What kind of support will I get after I get licensed?

When you join KW Legacy Group, you'll be connected with a Productivity Coach who mentors you one-on-one through your first six transactions. You'll also have access to world-class KW training, a collaborative team around you, and leadership that actually picks up the phone. Most new agents feel completely on their own after getting licensed — that's not how we do things here.
